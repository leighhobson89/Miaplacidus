# Playtest Session

## Session Summary

Played the local MIAPLACIDUS build for at least 30 minutes (09:14:22–09:44:22 UTC on 2026-10-04) in a new Chrome tab. Started a fresh `playTest-20261004` pioneer with $10, 50 Research Points, 0 Hydrogen, and 150 capacity. A cross-tab save warning appeared; I preserved the run through Save Manager as `playTest-20261004-2` without touching the primary game tab or deleting/reloading a save. Later, Save Manager listed `playTest-20261004` alongside the older `Pioneer` slot.

Progressed through the first Hydrogen resource loop, bought two compressors and four Science Kits, unlocked Knowledge Sharing and Fusion Theory, and reviewed achievements, autobuyers, saving, and the getting-started guide. At the end, the active run showed 150/150 Hydrogen, two compressors, four Science Kits, 465 Research Points after researching two technologies, and $24.10.

## Player Journey

1. The opening screen clearly asked for a pioneer name and language. I kept English and confirmed `playTest-20261004`; the first briefing said to collect Hydrogen, sell it, and use Hydrogen to expand storage or build a compressor.
2. I collected 5 Hydrogen manually. The UI showed a sale value of $0.10 at $0.02 each; selling the batch raised cash from $10.00 to $10.10.
3. After collecting to 10 Hydrogen, the game showed “This save changed in another tab. Reload or export this run before saving.” The Save Manager showed the active `playTest-20261004` run and only the old `Pioneer` in the slot list. I chose an unused name, `playTest-20261004-2`, with “Save run as a new pioneer.” The warning disappeared. A later Save Manager view showed `playTest-20261004` plus `Pioneer`, confirming the originally requested `playTest-...` slot was saved. I canceled out without reloading, overwriting, copying again, or deleting.
4. I collected to 50 Hydrogen, which unlocked the “Collect 50 Hydrogen” achievement and gave $10 this run. I spent the 50 Hydrogen on a compressor. The card said it adds 2 Hydrogen/s, while the displayed production became +4/s. A second compressor cost 57 Hydrogen; after buying it, the displayed rate became +8/s while the card still said 2/s.
5. The storage cap was 150. The resource inventory showed an Autobuyers panel with higher tiers locked by Quantum Computing and Rocket Composites; the displayed buy option for tier 3 required 2,000 Hydrogen. At full storage, the “Collect 1 Hydrogen” button was disabled and the UI offered a 149 Hydrogen upgrade from 150 to 300 capacity.
6. I bought a Science Kit for $5, then a second for $6. Each showed +0.5 RP/s; the total displayed 1 RP/s. After navigating to Research, the page showed 1,365.05 Research Points even though the header had remained at 50. Knowledge Sharing was ready at 150 RP. I researched it, received the $30 “Research a Technology” achievement reward, then researched Fusion Theory for 750 RP. The remaining balance was 465.05.
7. I checked the Achievements page, getting-started guide, Game Options, saving settings, and Save Manager. I bought two more Science Kits ($7 and $8), reaching four owned and +2 RP/s. The final Hydrogen view showed 150/150 in all panels after switching away and back.

## What Worked Well

- The first-voyage briefing gives a clear initial loop and points to sensible next actions.
- Collection and sale feedback is concrete: the Hydrogen total and sale value update, and the app confirms “Hydrogen collected” and “Hydrogen sold.”
- The 50-Hydrogen achievement provides a noticeable $10 boost. The Achievements page clearly lists the reward and distinguishes “This run” from permanent rewards.
- Compressor and Science Kit costs are visible next to their effects, and Research has separate Research, Technology, and Tech Tree views. The tech tree clearly shows prerequisites and readiness.
- Save Manager’s “Save run as a new pioneer” let me preserve the in-progress run without replacing the older `Pioneer` slot.
- The terminal-style space presentation is consistent and readable, and the first screen is not crowded with later systems.

## Problems Found

### Save warning in a separate tab — confirmed once; not retriggered

- **What happened:** In a new tab on the plain local game URL, I started `playTest-20261004`, collected and sold 5 Hydrogen, then collected to 10. The warning “This save changed in another tab. Reload or export this run before saving” appeared. Another game tab was open, but I did not interact with it.
- **Expected:** A new pioneer in a separate tab would not immediately be considered changed by another tab, or the warning would identify which save/tab changed.
- **Actual:** Save Manager listed only the older `Pioneer` slot while the current run heading was `playTest-20261004`. The warning made it unclear whether the new run had been saved. Saving the run under an unused name removed the warning; the later slot list showed `playTest-20261004` and `Pioneer`.
- **Reproduction:** Start a new playTest pioneer in a second tab, collect 5 Hydrogen and sell it, then collect to 10. The warning appeared once in this session; I did not make a second new run to try to retrigger it.
- **Impact:** Medium. A player may reload and lose progress or believe the new slot does not exist. The safe recovery was available but not obvious from the warning alone.

### Compressor rate description and displayed output disagree — reproduced with two purchases

- **What happened:** After buying one compressor, the resource UI showed +4 Hydrogen/s. After buying a second, it showed +8/s. Each compressor card said “Adds 2 Hydrogen per second while active.”
- **Expected:** The card’s stated rate and the total production display should agree, or distinguish a base/tick multiplier.
- **Actual:** The total shown was double the per-compressor description at both one and two owned.
- **Reproduction:** Buy one compressor for 50 Hydrogen, then a second for 57; compare each card to the Hydrogen production total.
- **Impact:** Low to medium. It makes production and upgrade value hard to estimate; I could not determine which rate is authoritative.

### Resource and Research counters temporarily disagreed — likely stale display

- **What happened:** At one point the Hydrogen sidebar and inventory showed 150/150 and 0/s, while the main Hydrogen panel still showed 50/150 and +8/s. The collect button was disabled and the sale value was $3.00, matching 150 Hydrogen. Switching to Research and back made the main panel show 150/150. Similarly, the Research header stayed at 50 while the Research page showed 1,365.05; after researching Knowledge Sharing, header and page both showed 1,215.
- **Expected:** Every visible count for the same resource would show the same current total.
- **Actual:** The visible panels were inconsistent until navigation refreshed them.
- **Reproduction:** The mismatch occurred once after the run had progressed in the background; switching sections and returning corrected it. I did not reload the page.
- **Impact:** Medium. A player could delay a purchase or spend a resource based on a stale value.

### Passive-output timing was hard to judge in this browser session — uncertain cause

- **What happened:** Several 30–60 second waits left the visible Hydrogen and Research amounts unchanged while their producers displayed positive rates. Later, Hydrogen filled to 150 while I was navigating the UI, and Research showed 1,365.05 after I switched to the Research page. I could not isolate whether the earlier waits occurred while the tab was background-throttled or whether the views were stale.
- **Expected:** The UI would make clear whether production continues while another tab is active and would refresh totals predictably when returning.
- **Actual:** Output appeared to accumulate, but the counters did not consistently refresh during the waits.
- **Impact:** Low, but it makes “wait for production” feel unreliable. This needs a repeat on a clearly foregrounded tab before calling it a game-rule defect.

## UX and Clarity

- The “Hydrogen briefing” returned once after being dismissed and after selling the first small batch. I dismissed it again; it did not immediately return on the next collection. I could not reproduce the return a second time.
- The producer control is labeled “Pause” while active and changes to “Resume” when paused. This reads as the available action, but it does not directly state the current status; I toggled it once to learn the convention and restored it to active.
- The first resource panels encourage collection and selling, but the player must manually click “Collect 1 Hydrogen” to reach the first 50-Hydrogen compressor. This is clear but repetitive.
- The Save Manager recovery was workable once discovered, but a warning about changes “in another tab” did not explain whether the new pioneer itself was affected.

## Balance and Progression

- The first 50 Hydrogen requires repeated manual collection, but the $10 achievement reward softens the early cash constraint and makes the milestone feel worthwhile.
- The first compressor changes the loop, and a second compressor is affordable after another 57 Hydrogen. With two compressors, storage reached 150; at full capacity the rate displayed +0/s until space is expanded.
- The first capacity increase costs 149 Hydrogen for a 150-to-300 change, consuming nearly a full starting-capacity stock. The player can sell instead for cash or reinvest in storage, which is a clear choice.
- Research starts with 50 RP. A $5 Science Kit shows +0.5 RP/s; additional kits cost $6, $7, then $8. Knowledge Sharing costs 150 RP, and Fusion Theory costs 750 RP after Knowledge Sharing. The progression gives a clear next target once a player finds the Technology tab.
- The system has meaningful early decisions between compressors, storage, and research, but the compressor-rate mismatch and stale counters undermine confidence in those choices.

## Friction and Repetition

- Clicking a one-unit Hydrogen button 50 times to reach the first compressor is repetitive. The first compressor arrives soon enough to feel like a reward, but an active automation rate should clearly begin working and update totals.
- Save conflict recovery required opening Portable save code, naming a second run, and later verifying the slot list. The path was safe but more involved than expected for a fresh pioneer.
- Returning between pages to refresh stale totals added avoidable steps.

## Interesting Player Behaviour

- I used the visible “Save run as a new pioneer” action with an unused `playTest-...` name when the cross-tab warning appeared. This preserved the active run without reloading or overwriting the older slot.
- First reaching 50 Hydrogen awarded $10 once; a second collection to 50 did not pay the same reward again.
- A full 150-Hydrogen store showed no production and disabled manual collection, while the Hydrogen storage upgrade was available for 149.
- The Autobuyers panel showed tier 2 locked by Quantum Computing, tier 3 at 2,000 Hydrogen, and tier 4 locked by Rocket Composites.

## Questions for the Owner

- Is each Hydrogen Compressor intended to add 2 H₂/s, or should one and two compressors display +4/s and +8/s? Which displayed rate should players plan around?
- Should producers continue accumulating while the game tab is in the background? If so, should the page update its totals immediately on return?
- Is the cross-tab warning expected when a new pioneer is started in a second tab, even when the existing tab is on another pioneer?

## Suggested Follow-up

- Retest the cross-tab warning with a second fresh playTest slot and verify whether it depends on the other tab’s selected pioneer or autosave state. Keep the safe “save as new pioneer” recovery available.
- Recheck resource and RP counters on a clearly foregrounded browser tab after waiting, after switching sections, and after the tab returns from the background.
- Compare the compressor card and total production after one and two purchases; verify whether the intended rate is 2, 4, or 8 H₂/s.
- Continue the research path beyond Fusion Theory and investigate the 2,000-Hydrogen autobuyer tier if the next run reaches it.
