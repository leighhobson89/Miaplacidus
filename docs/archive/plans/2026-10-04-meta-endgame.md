# Meta endgame contract — Black Hole and megastructures

**Status:** completed and archived 4 October 2026. Covers Phase 5 tasks G-31–G-42. Source of truth is the current Cosmic Forge implementation and `docs/audit/foundation-meta.md`; old GDD battle prose is not implemented as a separate Master AI encounter without source evidence.

## Black Hole (G-31–G-35)

- After the first rebirth (the second run), each completed normal star study adds 3 percentage points to discovery probability, capped at 100%. The new probability is rolled immediately; the first eligible study therefore has a 3% chance. A successful roll permanently unlocks research.
- Research costs 1,000,000 research points. It permanently unlocks charge and upgrades and immediately starts a charge.
- Base charge duration is 300,000 ms; minimum is 30,000 ms. A recharge purchase multiplies the recharge factor by 0.88, clamped to `30,000 / 300,000`; completing that final step makes the hole continuously active.
- Base time-warp duration is 3,000 ms and increases by 3,000 ms per duration purchase. Power starts at 5 and each power purchase adds 2 until power reaches 50, then adds 0.5. Initial power/duration/recharge prices are 850,000/600,000/900,000 research points; each purchased price is `ceil(oldPrice * 1.13)`.
- Charge, telescope, research, travel, battle, casino and Cosmic Rip timers use the timer policy recorded in `src/engine/timers.ts`. In this slice, the Black Hole charge is simulation-time, offline-eligible at full wall-time rate, and warpable. The finite warp itself uses wall time, is not accelerated by itself, and expires while the app is in the background. The existing generic `timeWarp` state remains the shared multiplier/duration source for casino and Black Hole warps. Always-on Black Hole power applies only during foreground simulation.
- All completion state changes are applied at the engine boundary, are idempotent, and survive save/reload. Discovery/upgrades persist across rebirth; charge readiness/progress and an active finite warp do not. A researched non-always-on hole resumes charging after rebirth.

## Manuscripts and megastructures (G-36–G-39)

- Preserve the four manuscript slots and their ordered study thresholds: up to a 20% chance per eligible study below 5, 20, 35 and 45 light-years, with a guaranteed roll when a study first crosses the next threshold. Slot four is the Dyson Sphere clue. Factory stars are assigned in the respective 5–15, 16–25, 26–40 and 41–60 light-year bands; the first three slots receive one of the other three tracks. A manuscript and its factory are distinct, unique eligible stars. Exclude Miaplacidus, the current or initially-settled star, O-type stars and already-settled/assigned systems. The remake derives each slot and star choice from stable per-study seeds so loading or rendering the map does not consume gameplay randomness.
- The manuscript clue and its factory assignment are permanent but the factory remains undisclosed until the manuscript system is settled. Settling reports that clue once, presents its factory and structure, and the first reported clue doubles each compound's current amount once, capped at storage. Settling the first factory pays $1,000,000 once. Clue text follows positions 1–4. These are global first-claim rewards, not one reward per clue/factory.
- There are four independent five-stage tracks. The shared stage prices are 50,000/100,000/150,000/200,000/250,000 research; the first four tracks reveal those stages at 40,000/80,000/120,000/160,000/200,000 accumulated research, while the Archive reveal thresholds equal the prices. Entry prerequisites are `advancedPowerGeneration` (Dyson), `quantumComputing` (Core), `neutronCapture` (Forge), and `orbitalConstruction` (Archive); later stages chain from the previous stage. The assigned factory must be the current settled system. Research uses the ordinary immediate technology action; there is no separate construction timer in the current source path, so time warp does not accelerate these purchases.
- Stage-three research on each track disconnects one field segment and contributes 0.15/s to the permanent antimatter milestone rate; the fourth distinct track unlocks the field reward and its 100 AP achievement once. Dyson stages 1–2 double battery/energy capacity and multiply power-plant rates by 1.25; stage 3 adds antimatter; stage 4 enables infinite power and stage 5 captures the structure while retaining it. The Core adds local research-rate bonuses of 0.5, 1, and 1.5 at stages 1, 2 and 4; stage 3 adds antimatter; stage 5 adds 2 locally or 5 away and captures it. Forge production multipliers are 1.25, 1.5 and 1.75 at stages 1, 2 and 4; stage 3 adds antimatter; stage 5 applies its 2x rate and 5x permanent rate modifier (full track product 32.8125x). Archive stages 1, 2, 4 and 5 add 100,000, 1,000,000, 1,000,000,000 and 10,000,000,000 capacity respectively to every resource and compound; stage 3 adds antimatter. Permanent researched stage IDs restore their bonuses exactly once on a new run.
- Factory stars and O-type systems receive deterministic robotic, extreme, war-ready encounters. The Miaplacidus Warden encounter is robotic/extreme with 100 air, land and sea units and the `aiMasterRace` anomaly. A lost fight destroys the attacking fleet, leaves surviving defenders in place and keeps the ship in orbit so the player can rebuild and retry.

The megastructure browser route uses a deterministic late-game fixture: it finishes the fourth field milestone, asserts the 100 AP boundary reward is paid once, then reaches Miaplacidus through the normal map selection, ship launch, travel timer, scan, war, battle and settlement controls. It advances the four-page homecoming story. This is the source-supported Miaplacidus route; the separate `completeGame` flag belongs to the Cosmic Rip closure sequence.
