# Cosmic Rip contract — G-43–G-50

**Status:** completed and archived 4 October 2026. Source observations are in [`docs/audit/foundation-meta.md`](../../audit/foundation-meta.md). The remake uses the Miaplacidus homecoming acknowledgement to unlock the Cosmic Rip tab; this is an intentional route gate in place of the source build's technology flag.

## Source rules

- The Near Space Scanner Array costs 10 GP to restore after Miaplacidus is settled. Restoration is one-time and seeds a single uniform Rip sector.
- There are nine sectors. Scanning an unscanned sector is immediate and costs 1 GP; scanning the seeded sector finds the Rip. The source does not time sector scans.
- A Sensor Buoy costs $500,000, 100,000 Titanium and 600,000 Silicon, and adds 0.04 telemetry per second. A Rip Research Orbiter costs $1,000,000, 1,000,000 Helium, 1,000,000 Sodium and 500,000 Steel, and adds 0.07/s. Each purchase scales every cash/material cost by `ceil(previous × 1.13)`. Telemetry has no gameplay storage cap.
- The five ordered technologies are Stabilizer Array (5,000 reveal / 10,000 telemetry / 60 s), Quantum Containment Field (12,000 / 15,000 / 120 s), Dimensional Anchor Matrix (16,000 / 25,000 / 180 s), Singularity Stabilizer (30,000 / 40,000 / 240 s), and Reality Weave Regulator (40,000 / 60,000 / 300 s). A reveal threshold is not the research price. Every stage also costs 1 GP and requires its predecessor, except the first.
- The current source gives these five research stages no modifier effects outside Cosmic Rip: their behavior is sequential research progress, and the last stage enables closure. The remake therefore does not invent a cross-system modifier.
- Closing requires all five technologies, costs 1 GP, and is one-time. The source sets both `closeCosmicRip` and `completeGame`; the achievements themselves have narrative-only rewards.
- Cosmic Rip progress, including telemetry, purchases, research progress and completion, persists across rebirth. Source timers advance during ordinary simulation and the source offline handler.

## Remake behavior and verification

- State is permanently owned in `permanent.cosmicRip`; migration v25→v26 initializes empty progress. Story acknowledgement unlocks the tab. The scanner restore and each sector scan use exact GP charges. The seeded location is saved and cannot reroll after reload.
- The purchase catalogue uses escalating source prices. Telemetry is generated by source rates during foreground simulation, warp and offline steps. Research spends telemetry and GP when started, and stores its active technology and elapsed time in save state; progress continues across reload and rebirth.
- Closing sets the durable endgame flag. The Cosmic Rip pane is translated for `en`, `es`, `pt`, `de`, `it`, and `fr`.
- Focused unit coverage: `tests/unit/cosmic-rip.spec.ts` and `tests/unit/local-saves.spec.ts`. The browser journey in `tests/e2e/cosmic-rip/cosmic-rip.spec.ts` exercises normal UI research and closure, save/reload, and the translated Cosmic Rip surface in all six shipped locales.
- Focused results: `npm.cmd run test:unit:focused -- tests/unit/cosmic-rip.spec.ts` passed 12 tests; Chrome `npm.cmd run test:e2e:focused -- tests/e2e/cosmic-rip/cosmic-rip.spec.ts` passed 1 route after status styling was aligned to its snapshot. The browser run used the Chrome channel because the bundled Chromium binary is not installed. `npm.cmd run typecheck` passed. The once-only G-43–G-50 unit suite passed 20 files / 207 tests; fixes after that run were checked only in affected areas.

## Intentional differences and limits

- The source tab is gated by a Cosmic Rip technology/build flag. This remake gates it on the Miaplacidus story acknowledgement so the shipped route itself unlocks the ending system without inventing a standard research technology.
- The source presents an ending modal/cinematic; the remake currently records and displays the completed end state in the tab. Cinematic presentation assets remain part of Phase 6.
- No separate construction materials or timer are invented for the scanner, sector scan, or telemetry upgrades. The timed actions are the five source research stages.
