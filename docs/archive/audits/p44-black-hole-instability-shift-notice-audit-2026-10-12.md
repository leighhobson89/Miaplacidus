# P-44 Black Hole Instability shift notice audit

**Date:** 12 October 2026  
**Scope:** Player-facing Black Hole Instability result notices in `en`, `es`, `pt`, `de`, `it`, and `fr`.

Cosmic Forge `events.js:667-738` applies an initial Black Hole Instability shift when the event starts, then applies another shift each active minute. Each shift calls `showNotification` with `notificationBlackHoleInstabilityShift` when the Black Hole is always on, or `notificationBlackHoleInstabilityShiftWithDuration` otherwise. The localized `{powerText}` and `{durationText}` values come from `textInstabilityStable`, `textInstabilityWeaker`, and `textInstabilityStronger` (with `textInstabilityUnknown` as a defensive fallback) in `localization.json`.

MIAPLACIDUS already applied the same initial and minute-based multipliers in `src/engine/randomEvents.ts`, but exposed only trigger and expiry events. It had no event or notification route for the changed values, so players received no shift result notice. Added `random-event.black-hole-instability-shifted` events for the initial and recurring shifts, a localized renderer in `src/i18n/randomEventInstabilityNotificationMessages.ts`, and a five-second informational notice route in `src/app/randomEventInstabilityNotifications.ts` wired from `src/app/App.tsx`. Notices respect the existing notification preference. Always-on Black Holes show the source power-only variant; other Black Holes show power and duration. The multiplier delta wording and all six locale templates match the source keys; percentage values retain Cosmic Forge's rounded integer calculation.

The engine now follows the source expiry-first order: when a tick expires Black Hole Instability, it emits the ended event and returns without applying accumulated minute shifts or consuming their random rolls. This prevents a shift notice from appearing on the same expiry tick as the stabilization notice. While the event remains active, the existing shift timing, multiplier values, and random-roll behavior are unchanged. Source behavior was compared against `events.js:1036-1058` and all six `localization.json` locale blocks; remake ownership was checked through the engine event union and global `NotificationStack` route. No tests, builds, typechecks, formatting, or browser checks were run. P-44 remains open for wider rendered-notice verification; expiry-modal implementation evidence is in the [expiry result-dialog audit](p44-random-event-expiry-dialog-audit-2026-10-12.md).
