# Presentation navigation contract

**Status:** active for M-06. This contract records what the source places in each top-level tab and how the remake will expose the same information hierarchy. Source observations come from Cosmic Forge commit `93e32669c3b35e76cdd4cf82725c14e7215b2fbc`; redesign choices are labeled below.

## Navigation model

- Preserve the owner's stable tab sequence, with **Compounds pinned directly after Resources whenever unlocked**, followed by the remaining tabs in their declaration order; append MIAPLACIDUS's new `Miaplaedia` after Settings. Cosmic Forge's runtime does reorder unlocked tabs by a priority table (`ui.js:10370-10400`; fully unlocked order is Resources, Compounds, Research, Energy, Space Mining, Interstellar, Galactic, Cosmic Rip, Settings). The owner updated the remake order on 6 October 2026 to put Compounds second. Filter locked tabs without reordering the other survivors. Render tab names only; do not display source numbers or numeric prefixes.
- Each of the nine source gameplay tabs is a domain whose source menu entries are child panes, not new top-level tabs. Miaplaedia is the added guide/reference tab.
- Use stable, language-independent IDs for tabs, panes, and actions. Localized labels are presentation only.
- Keep the active child pane when the player visits another top-level tab and returns. A pane change must not reset a running simulation task or discard values typed into a child view.
- Omit locked main gameplay tabs entirely. Compounds occupies the second visible position after Resources once available; filter locked tabs while preserving the order of the other tabs. Settings remains the last original source tab, and Miaplaedia follows it. Do not render locked-tab placeholders or explanations.
- Keep locked child pages hidden until unlocked. Settings and Miaplaedia remain available at all times.
- Mark destinations that become available during the current session with a localized `New` badge. Their parent tab inherits the badge until the player opens each new child pane; selecting a destination clears its badge. Preserve live source status indicators separately from novelty: when Black Hole research is complete and its charge is ready, show a localized ready badge on the Black Hole child page and Galactic parent tab until the time warp starts.
- On narrow screens, keep child-pane navigation visible and reachable without hover. It may scroll horizontally; the page itself must not overflow horizontally.
- Do not expose source entries excluded by product decisions, such as cloud transfer, original-game save import, or Electron exit.

## Deliberate presentation decisions

These differences are owner-directed and do not imply that an unlisted source feature has been intentionally removed:

- **Progression tabs:** keep the source's stable destination order, but omit destinations that are locked in the active run. Do not show locked tabs disabled or renumber the remaining tabs. This keeps the map consistent while showing only places the player can currently use.
- **Tab labels:** show the destination names without source index numbers. Keep source option IDs internally so routing and parity checks remain stable.
- **Galactic Casino:** keep the Casino as its own full Galactic child page in the observed source order, after Galactic Market and before Ascendency Perks. Its statistics remain a distinct summary group in Settings → Statistics.
- **Reference documents:** move the source's seven complete guide documents from Settings to the Miaplaedia tab. Preserve all six localized source texts with only the approved game and player name changes: Cosmic Forge becomes Miaplacidus, Cosmicopedia becomes Miaplaedia, and the player is Mia'Plac.
- **Startup:** the one-screen first-run Hydrogen briefing panel and replay button are permanently removed by owner decision; a new or resumed run enters gameplay directly. Progression-wide tutorial and step-tracking parity remains open under P-36.
- **Pioneer selection:** use a searchable picker. When the last-started save is loadable, preselect it so the filled pioneer name offers `RESUME GAME <Name>` immediately. Other matching saves require an explicit click; an unselected name starts a new game. Remove the old save-name table and Confirm-then-Start step.
- **Top status strip:** use source stats 2–8 and omit stat 1 because cash is already visible in the game header. Keep the source information and tooltips rather than duplicating cash.
- **Technology navigation:** keep a Research child page for science buildings and automation, and a separate Tech Tree child page containing only the left-to-right graph. Each tech becomes visible once its reveal condition is met and remains visible after points are spent; prerequisites continue to control whether it can be researched. Keep scrolling, zoom, and detail tooltips.
- **News ticker:** keep the ticker in the game shell rather than adding a tab. Hover does not pause it; clickable items use the current theme's ready color without underlines. After a prize is claimed, keep its localized `here` word visible at 50% opacity and disable further clicks rather than replacing it with `Claimed`.
- **Resource page headings:** remove the redundant visible `Resources` heading and `Hydrogen starts unlocked` line above the resource content; the selected tab and child page already provide that context.
- **Theme and type:** match the source's Terminal black and lime palette with white accents, and use a terminal-style font for that theme. Other themes keep their own palette and typography.

The checklist records remaining work separately: responsive and theme review, full tutorial steps, complete Statistics metric parity, and broad browser/accessibility review remain open. The decisions above are not a waiver for those items.

**Statistics section placement:** Statistics remains one child destination under Settings. Inside it, preserve the source's separate Events, Galactic Casino, and Cosmic Rip Chapter summary groups. Those are summary groups only: the full event history stays under Settings → Events, the full Casino page remains a Galactic child, and Cosmic Rip gameplay remains in its own main tab. The remake renders current-run and persistent all-time values for thirteen random-event and ten Galactic Casino counters, plus Cosmic Rip telemetry, scan, research, and progress status. The remaining source Statistics rows are part of the open P-55 screen-coverage review.
