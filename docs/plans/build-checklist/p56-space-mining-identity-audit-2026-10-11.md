# P-56 Space Mining page identity audit (11 October 2026)

Compared the Space Mining child pages, source order/ownership, rocket visibility, telescope timer, and precipitation placement. No player-visible mismatch was found, so no implementation change was made.

- **Source ownership and order:** Cosmic Forge lists Mining, Space Telescope, Asteroids, Launch Pad, then Rocket 1-4 as separate options within Space Mining (`cosmicForge/cosmicForge/index.html:565-656`). Their click handlers route to matching page headings (`ui.js:10848-10926`), and the content renderer has distinct sections for Mining, Telescope, Asteroids, and Launch Pad (`drawTab6Content.js:61-91,619,849,1121`). MIAPLACIDUS exposes separate child IDs in that same visible order; Mining appears first once antimatter is unlocked, while Telescope is first before that, followed by Asteroids, Launch Pad, and each available rocket (`src/app/presentationNavigation.ts:455-515`). Each selected child renders one large heading using its navigation label; there is no duplicate Space Mining page heading (`src/app/SpaceMiningPane.tsx:600-682`; `App.tsx:2777-2800`).
- **Rocket pages and names:** Cosmic Forge adds a rocket to `rocketsBuilt` at its required part count (`game.js:10282-10286`) and only shows its sidebar row when that ID is present (`ui.js:8566-8579`). MIAPLACIDUS likewise reveals each Rocket N page only after the Launch Pad is built and that rocket reaches its required part count. The child label and heading follow the saved custom name, with a localized Rocket N fallback (`presentationNavigation.ts:493-515`; `SpaceMiningPane.tsx:600-603`).
- **Space Telescope identity and timer:** the page title is Space Telescope and its artwork kind is `radio-telescope` (`SpaceMiningPane.tsx:606,675-682`). While a survey/search timer is active, the page shows remaining time and a native progress bar whose value follows elapsed timer progress (`SpaceMiningPane.tsx:736-769`).
- **Precipitation placement:** the app renders precipitation rate and collected-this-run only inside the location/weather tooltip in `TopStatusBar.tsx:413-425`; no other app renderer contains either player-facing line. `SpaceMiningPane.tsx` does not display those fields, including on Launch Pad.

This was a static source/render review. No tests, builds, typechecks, formatting, or browser checks were run.
