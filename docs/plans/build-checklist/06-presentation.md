## News Ticker source parity

If it is absent in a playthrough, check that saved toggle first. Keep its behavior aligned with Cosmic Forge (`cosmicForge/cosmicForge/game.js`, `ui.js`, and `descriptions.js`): rotate messages on the source's randomized 20–35 second interval; use the source mix of 3% one-off rewards, 10% resource prizes, 15% wacky prompts, and ordinary headlines otherwise; on ordinary headlines, offer an eligible manuscript clue 25% of the time. Prize headlines must show the exact claimable amount and the inline `here` action must award that amount; after claiming, keep the word `here`, disable further clicks, and show it at 50% opacity instead of replacing it with a claimed label. Type-B star bonuses affect resource autobuyer production, not ticker prize amounts. One-off items must only be claimable once. Manuscript clues must refer to eligible, unreported manuscripts and record which clues were shown. All eight source wacky prompts (wave, disco, bounce, fade, glitch, wobble, boo, feedback) must be clickable and run their matching effect until the next message replaces it; preserve seen, claimed, clue, and activated state for the Events history and related statistics/achievements.
