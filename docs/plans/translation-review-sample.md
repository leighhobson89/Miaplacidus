# Six-language translation review sample

Prepared for the project owner's quick quality review. These are current player-facing strings selected to cover startup, the core loop, a save failure, travel, and the ending. No owner approval is recorded yet.

The sample reads from `saveText(startNewGame)`, `settingsHelpText(early)`, `saveErrorText("quota")`, `starshipText(launch)`, and `miaplacidusStoryText(locale)`.

## English

- **Startup:** START NEW GAME
- **Core loop:** Collect Hydrogen to begin, then sell stock for cash. Each material has its own storage cap; the rate shows how quickly production and use change its stock.
- **Save failure:** Browser storage is full. The previous save remains available; export or remove a save to make room.
- **Travel:** Launch starship
- **Ending:** Miaplacidus Reclaimed — The Wardens' defeat breaks their hold over Miaplacidus. The sealed sky opens, and your people can return to the world they carried in memory.

## Spanish

- **Error al guardar:** El almacenamiento está lleno. La partida anterior sigue disponible; exporta o elimina una partida.
- **Viaje:** Lanzar nave estelar
- **Final:** Miaplacidus recuperado — La derrota de los Guardianes termina su dominio sobre Miaplacidus. El cielo sellado se abre y tu pueblo puede regresar al mundo que conservó en su memoria.
