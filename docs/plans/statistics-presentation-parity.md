**Status: Active (P-55).** This plan resolves the remaining source Statistics rows while keeping live gameplay readouts in their owning pages and preserving honest run/lifetime history. The [P-55 row audit](presentation-navigation-contract.md#statistics-source-row-audit-open-p-55) records 102 source rows in 11 source groups. Focused unit and Chrome verification for Research passed on 6 October; the subsequent Energy and Interstellar slices are recorded below. A card-count match is not the acceptance criterion: every source row must map to the correct value and scope.

## Metrics with ambiguous or inconsistent source semantics

Leave these mappings unresolved until the owner chooses the intended meaning; do not encode an interpretation as parity:

- **Enemy Total Defence Overcome:** the source marks the current-run value Not Applicable, while its all-time getter calculates `max(0, enemyFleetPowerAtBattleStart - currentDestinationFleetPower)`. This is a single battle's start-to-current difference, not a cumulative sum across enemies or runs. Preserve the source observation and seek the intended statistic before adding a counter.
- **Galactic Points Earned:** the source all-time getter uses `max(0, settledSystemCount - 1)` as a proxy, while the remake awards `1 + expansionistExtraSystemIds.length` GP per rebirth in `src/engine/metaProgression.ts`. The proxy does not constitute an explicit GP-earned ledger. Leave the Statistics value open pending owner direction on whether to preserve the proxy or represent awarded GP.
- **Galactic Casino outcomes:** the source Higher/Lower “Won” counter and remake semantics differ at the deck-completion edge. Keep the parity decision open until the owner chooses the intended outcome definition. Cosmic Forge also increments Double-or-Nothing and Wheel “Played” counters for invalid direct calls before validation; no mismatch through ordinary UI play has been established, so do not present that edge case as a confirmed player-facing remake defect.
- **Cosmic Rip metric meaning and scope:** all seven source Run values are Not Applicable and the source All Time getters are listed in the audit above. The remake presents one value per card; the intended display scopes and meanings remain unresolved pending the owner's async choice. Do not label these cards as current-run/all-time parity until this is resolved. Translation-quality approval for the six-locale labels and tracking note also remains pending.
